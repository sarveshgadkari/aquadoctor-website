About ADS

End-to-end aquaculture support, from quality inputs to market linkage

We collaborate with fish farmers, hatcheries and aquaculture input partners to promote sustainable practices, healthy fish production and improved farmer livelihoods.

Founded by Dr. Debtanu Barman, widely known as the “Aqua Doctor”, ADS brings together a team of young professionals who blend modern technology with professional ethics, giving farmers and entrepreneurs scientifically proven, high-yield fish farming techniques.

Success story

Solar aerators in Kajla village

With support from IITG–TIDF, ADS introduced solar-powered aerators in Kajla village, enabling 25 farmers to adopt climate-resilient aquaculture practices.

Reliable oxygen supply without electricity or fuel, reducing costs, improving fish survival and supporting sustainable, low-carbon livelihoods.
