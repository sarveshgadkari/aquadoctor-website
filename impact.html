Results from the field

What the work has achieved

Figures and outcomes as reported in the Aqua Doctor Solutions Annual Report 2025.

Climate resilient aquaculture

Solar-powered aerators in Kajla village

25farmers adopted climate-resilient practices

With support from IITG–TIDF, ADS introduced solar-powered aerators in Kajla village. The intervention ensures reliable oxygen supply without electricity or fuel, reducing costs, improving fish survival and supporting sustainable, low-carbon livelihoods.

Aquatic livelihood

Steadier incomes for coastal families

120coastal families in West Bengal

ADS strengthened aquatic livelihoods by promoting scientific and sustainable aquaculture practices. Through training and field support, 120 coastal families improved productivity, reduced risks and achieved more stable incomes.

Aqua enterprise development

Supporting women entrepreneurs in aquatic livelihoods

Together with micro and small enterprises, and with Community-Based Organisations, Self-Help Groups, Fisheries Cooperatives and Farmers' Producer Organisations, ADS fosters inclusive economic growth and gender empowerment.

Fish farming training, handholding support and enterprise-oriented solutions help strengthen women-led businesses and their economic independence.

Access to finance

Insurance and financial support

25farmers insured with National Insurance

ADS facilitated aqua insurance coverage to protect fish farmers from disease outbreaks, natural calamities and production losses. The organisation has also received financial support from government and semi-government organisations, such as NABARD, to scale up its interventions.

FFPOs and SHGs

2,000+ farmers and 10 FPCs/FFPOs

Technical, enterprise and market support that promotes scientific aquaculture and collective growth across the ADS network.

Fishery institutes and KVKs

MoUs with 5+ institutes and KVKs

Technical support, training and field demonstrations to promote scientific and climate-resilient aquaculture.

Aqua Learning

100+ sessions last year

Online and offline training at institutes on fisheries and employment, promoting aquapreneurship development.

Value-added products

10+ products, engaging 20+ women

Fish value-added products that increase income and reduce post-harvest losses, with hygienic processing and enterprise development.

Desalination water filtration

Safe drinking water in the Sundarbans

A desalination filtration system that provides safe, affordable drinking water and reduces the financial burden on households in salinity-affected areas.

Community engagement through aquaculture

Adopt the Village in East Midnapore

Kajla Janakalyan Samity (KJKS) and Aqua Doctor Solutions, in collaboration with Upakulia Primary Matsyajeebi Samabay Samity Ltd. (UPMSS), run a transformative initiative in East Medinipur, West Bengal, to revolutionise sustainable aquaculture and rural livelihoods.

Despite the region's vast inland water resources, only part of them are productively utilised. The project's core mission is to enhance incomes, create jobs and establish a climate-resilient aquaculture value chain through eco-friendly technologies.
