Who we are

Aqua Doctor Solutions, established on 14th July 2019, works actively in the East and North-Eastern states of India, providing end-to-end aquaculture support — quality inputs, fish health management, water quality solutions, technical advisory, consultation and farm-level guidance, up to market linkage.

We collaborate with fish farmers, hatcheries and aquaculture input partners to promote sustainable practices, healthy fish production and better farmer livelihoods. Our team of young professionals blends modern technology with professional ethics to give farmers and entrepreneurs scientifically proven, high-yield fish farming techniques.

Our work contributes to sustainable fish production, reduces pressure on capture fisheries and advances the small-pond ecosystem — in line with the Blue Economy and the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

The “Aqua Doctor”

Dr. Debtanu Barman

Founder and Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Debtanu Barman is a young aquapreneur and aquaculture consultant from Kolkata, West Bengal, widely recognised as the “Aqua Doctor” for his pioneering contributions to the aquaculture industry. He specialises in expert advice, technology transfer and aquaculture farm management — bringing modern technologies and best management practices to existing farms to raise productivity and sustainability.

He founded Aqua Doctor Solutions & Aqua Clinic in 2019 with his own investment. The company's growth was strengthened by a grant from the National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB), Hyderabad, facilitated through the National Institute of Agricultural Extension Management (MANAGE), Hyderabad, Government of India. He also trains students and farmers at institutes across the region on fisheries, employment and aquapreneurship.

“By embracing sustainability, innovation and perseverance, aquapreneurs can grow not only fish but also family prosperity.”

Our core service

Aquaculture technical support: Doctor-on-Call

Round-the-clock diagnostic and advisory support connecting farmers directly with our aquaculture experts. From rapid water testing and soil analysis to fish disease diagnosis, our “Doctor-on-Call” service brings a physical-and-digital (“phygital”) health management system straight to your pond — so problems are caught early and losses are prevented before they happen.

Call the doctor WhatsApp photos
  • Rapid water testing
  • Soil analysis
  • Fish disease diagnosis
  • Advice by phone, WhatsApp and farm visit
  • Follow-up until the pond recovers

Climate resilient aquaculture support

We help farmers future-proof their ponds against climate volatility through solar-powered aerators, solar pumps, and Biofloc-based systems that reduce dependence on unreliable power grids and diesel. These low-carbon, cost-efficient innovations keep oxygen and water quality stable even through erratic weather — protecting yields and reducing input costs.

Solar aeratorsSolar pumpsBiofloc systemsLower diesel use

In the field: with support from IITG–TIDF, 25 farmers in Kajla village now run solar-powered aerators.

Hi-tech aquaculture support

Real-time, data-driven pond management using IoT-enabled sensors that continuously monitor dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature, turbidity, ammonia, and water levels. Paired with drone-based monitoring and our Matsya Sathi digital platform, this gives farmers the ability to make timely, informed decisions and prevent losses before they occur.

Dissolved oxygenpHTemperatureTurbidityAmmoniaWater levelDrone monitoringMatsya Sathi

In the field: drone-based pond monitoring demonstrations with KVKs, and the Matsya Sathi app — India's first integrated aquaculture companion app.

Expert consultancy

Our team of aquaculture professionals provides end-to-end technical consultancy — covering farm design, species selection, feed and health management, and productivity optimisation — tailored to your farm's specific water body, geography, and goals.

Farm designSpecies selectionFeed managementHealth managementProductivity

Our approach: end-to-end support from inputs and fish health to farm-level guidance and market linkage.

Aquaculture and fisheries insurance

We help farmers secure their livelihoods against disease outbreaks, natural calamities, and production losses by facilitating access to government-backed and formal fisheries insurance schemes (including linkages with National Insurance and NFDB registration), giving farming families genuine financial protection alongside technical support.

Disease outbreaksNatural calamitiesProduction lossNFDB registration

In the field: 25 farmers received aqua insurance cover from National Insurance with ADS support.

DPR (Detailed Project Report) preparation

We prepare comprehensive, bank- and government-scheme-ready Detailed Project Reports for aquaculture ventures — covering feasibility, financials, and technical planning — helping farmers, entrepreneurs, and FPOs/FFPOs access institutional finance and government support with confidence.

FeasibilityFinancialsTechnical planningBank and scheme ready

Finance partners: support from government and semi-government organisations such as NABARD.

Aqua learning and training

Structured training and capacity-building programmes — delivered both online and in the field — covering scientific aquaculture practices, enterprise development, and aquapreneurship. Our sessions have reached thousands of farmers, FPCs, and FFPOs, building the knowledge base that turns traditional fish farming into a sustainable, scalable business.
