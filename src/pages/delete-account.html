How it works

Three steps to delete your account

Deleting your account is permanent. Your account, farm details and consultation history cannot be recovered afterwards.

  3. 03Your account is deletedWe delete your account and data within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

Your data

What is deleted and what we keep

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Deleted within 30 days

  • Your account: name, mobile number, email address and profile
  • Farm and pond details you entered
  • Photos, videos and messages you sent for diagnosis or advice
  • Consultation history, saved addresses and e-learning progress
  • Location and device details linked to your account

Kept only where the law requires

  • Invoices, order and payment records: kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting laws (usually up to 8 years), then deleted.
  • Insurance records: kept as long as the insurance company and insurance rules require.
  • Records of an open complaint or dispute: kept until it is resolved.
  • Backups: your data is removed from backup copies within 90 days.

Data that has been anonymised, so that it can no longer identify you, may be kept for research and service improvement.
