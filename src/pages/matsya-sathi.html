What the app does

Everything a fish farmer needs, in one place

Matsya Sathi brings the services of Aqua Doctor Solutions to every farmer's phone, in simple language.

  • E-learning

    Lessons and training on fish farming, pond preparation, feeding, water quality and fish health.

  • E-commerce

    Order quality seed, feed, medicines, test kits and equipment from ADS.

  • Doctor consultation

    Send photos of your fish and pond and get advice from aquaculture experts.

  • Market linkage

    Connect with buyers and markets to sell your harvest at a fair price.
