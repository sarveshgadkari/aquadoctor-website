Aquaculture technical support: Doctor-on-Call

Round-the-clock diagnostic and advisory support connecting farmers directly with our aquaculture experts. From rapid water testing and soil analysis to fish disease diagnosis, our “Doctor-on-Call” service brings a physical-and-digital (“phygital”) health management system straight to your pond, so problems are caught early and losses are prevented before they happen.

  • Rapid water testing
  • Soil analysis
  • Fish disease diagnosis
  • Physical and digital health management

02

Climate resilient aquaculture support

We help farmers future-proof their ponds against climate volatility through solar-powered aerators, solar pumps and Biofloc-based systems that reduce dependence on unreliable power grids and diesel.

These low-carbon, cost-efficient innovations keep oxygen and water quality stable even through erratic weather, protecting yields and reducing input costs.

Solar-powered aeratorsSolar pumpsBiofloc-based systems

In the field: with support from IITG–TIDF, ADS introduced solar-powered aerators in Kajla village, enabling 25 farmers to adopt climate-resilient aquaculture practices.

05

Aquaculture and fisheries insurance

We help farmers secure their livelihoods against disease outbreaks, natural calamities and production losses by facilitating access to government-backed and formal fisheries insurance schemes, including linkages with National Insurance and NFDB registration.

This gives farming families genuine financial protection alongside technical support.

25farmers received insurance coverage from National Insurance with ADS support
06

DPR (Detailed Project Report) preparation

We prepare comprehensive, bank- and government-scheme-ready Detailed Project Reports for aquaculture ventures, helping farmers, entrepreneurs and FPOs/FFPOs access institutional finance and government support with confidence.

  1. Section 01

    Feasibility

    Is the venture viable for this site and scale?

  2. Section 02

    Financials

  3. Section 03

Who it is for: farmers, entrepreneurs and FPOs/FFPOs seeking bank finance or government support.

Aqua learning and training

Structured training and capacity-building programmes, delivered both online and in the field, covering scientific aquaculture practices, enterprise development and aquapreneurship.

Our sessions have reached thousands of farmers, FPCs and FFPOs, building the knowledge base that turns traditional fish farming into a sustainable, scalable business.

100+online and offline training sessions last year
